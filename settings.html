Repositories belong to teams, below.

Teams

A team is a set of repos, and every active team's repos are charted together on the Activity tab. Its backlog is the stories titled with one of its prefixes or under its area paths; with neither, it's whatever the members are assigned. Members default to whoever has PRs in the repos. Switch a team off to leave it out without deleting it.

Work items

People

Who gets a row. Leave it empty to show the most active people Snoop finds.
